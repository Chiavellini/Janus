## Anchors checked
- 2019 Dorian: FSU-Boise State was a same-date venue+time change (not counted). 247Sports cited "a handful" of FCS moves; only the two cancellations were identifiable.
- 2021 Ida: Tulane-Oklahoma, Tulane-Morgan State and SELA-North Alabama were same-date venue changes; LSU-UCLA played. Temple-Rutgers is the only FBS date move.
- 2021 COVID: no FBS regular-season forfeit/cancellation; only Drake's forfeit in FCS.
- 2022 Ian: FSU-Wake Forest, Bethune-Cookman and FAMU games played as scheduled.
- 2023 Idalia/Ophelia, 2024 Francine, 2025 Imelda/Humberto/Erin: no D-I date changes found.
- 2024 Milton: UCF-Cincinnati and FAU-North Texas played as scheduled.

## Not confirmed / caveats
- Original kickoff times missing for six rows (SELA-BCU 2019, NSU-SELA 2021, Stetson-Drake 2021, UVA-VT 2022, Davidson-PC 2024, UC Davis-Mercer 2025).
- Davidson-Presbyterian also flipped host and venue; ESPN may list Davidson as home.
- Pre-season schedule changes (Fresno St-Sac St 2022, NMSU-NIU 2024) are excluded as not in-season.

## FCS completeness
FBS coverage is likely near-complete (each event appears in several national trackers). FCS coverage is roughly 70-80%: hurricane-week trackers and the 2023 NSU/Morgan State cases are well documented, but isolated single-game moves in SWAC/MEAC, NEC, Big Sky, Pioneer and Ivy play (lightning next-day resumptions, small-school COVID forfeits in fall 2021, the 2019 Dorian "handful") are poorly indexed by search; a few more FCS rows likely exist.
