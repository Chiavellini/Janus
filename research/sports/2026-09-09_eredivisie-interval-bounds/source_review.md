# Original bounds reviewed before final outcomes

The three official program PDFs archived under L49 are the June 24, 2025 definitive
program, the September 10 autumn program, and the December 23 spring program.
The publication pages link those exact attachments and identify their role and date.
June and September responses retain matching historical Last-Modified dates. The
spring KNVB download has no Last-Modified header; its dated publication and retained
conditional European placeholders are the available publication lineage, not a claim
that an HTTP URL is intrinsically immutable. Every acquired byte is hash-pinned.

The automatic join confirms every ordered pair of 18 clubs exactly once. June has
309 rows for 306 fixtures because three pairs have explicit alternatives. Those
alternatives do not enlarge the denominator. The first exact fixture dates come
from June for rounds 1–12 and 33–34, September for rounds 13–21, and December for
rounds 22–32. Earlier round windows are retained as source history but cannot be
treated as fixture-specific kickoffs.

All footnotes on the first-timing publications have been inspected. Their bound
treatment, fixed before final outcomes, is:

- June rounds 1, 2, 4, 5, 7–12 and spring round 27 allow clock permutations or
  adjustments. Local midnight is earlier than every possible same-day clock.
- June round 3 explicitly lists AZ–PEC on August 24 or September 24;
  Utrecht–Excelsior on August 24 or September 17; Feyenoord–Fortuna on September 17
  or September 24. Every row is retained and the earliest date controls.
- June round 7 permits Heracles–Sparta to move from September 28 to September 27
  at an unspecified time. September 27 midnight controls. Groningen–Feyenoord's
  September 27 original already precedes its possible September 28 alternative.
- Autumn round 11 allows the three specified cup-dependent clock/date permutations.
  The earlier September publication's Friday October 31 PSV–Fortuna date is retained
  even though June had Saturday November 1; Ajax–Heerenveen and Feyenoord–Volendam
  retain November 1 midnight. No later candidate can raise an earlier baseline.
- Autumn round 16 AZ–Excelsior and five round-20 fixtures may be postponed. The
  original December 14 / January 24–25 dates remain; December's later makeup dates
  never replace them. A possible later rescheduling is the event being measured.
- Spring round 30 Utrecht–Telstar and Groningen–Go Ahead may move from April 11
  to April 12 with times unknown. April 11 midnight controls. Round 32
  Utrecht–NAC may move May 2 to May 3, N.E.C.–Telstar May 2 to May 3, and
  AZ–Twente May 3 to May 2. May 2 midnight controls for all three.

Generic notices that future cup scheduling may cause further changes preserve the
already published baseline; they do not assert a separate earlier original date.
All table rows and all fixture-specific alternate days therefore have a bounded
earliest original. No fixture is silently deleted and none uses its eventual
result to choose an original. If later review exposes an unaccounted earlier
alternative, L50 can only become more conservative or stop.

The August 15 KNVB decision is deliberately not used to move an original later.
Its AZ–PEC makeup clock is 20:00, whereas the June alternative and September
program say 18:45. That inconsistency does not narrow the bound: August 24
midnight still precedes every cited candidate. It must remain visible for any
later exact-clock interpretation.

The 00:00 bound overcharges late next-day and second-day completions relative to
the real kickoff. Its expected EV effect is an increased settlement reserve,
deducted from executable net edge; it provides no positive-profit evidence.
