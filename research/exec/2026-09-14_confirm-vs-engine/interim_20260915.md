This is an interim read at eight eligible taker zero fills, not the registered approximately
50-order verdict.

All eight had complete `first_leg_confirm.sent` evidence showing at least the sent quantity at or
below the sent price. Every post-terminal read then showed zero depth at that price. Under L102's
immutable taxonomy the current split is therefore `CONSUMED=8`, `STILL_THERE=0`, `PULLED=0`.

remained unhonoured, but unrelated market flow could also consume the depth; the sample is small.
