# Janus snooping ledger

> **No backtest number exists outside this file.** If a result is not attached to a row here, it
> is not a result — it is a scratch calculation and must not be quoted, promoted, or carried into
> a decision.

## The rules

1. **One test look = one new numbered row.** A row is appended with `verdict = PENDING`
   **before** the run, and filled in after. Never the other way round.
2. **BH-FDR threshold = `0.1 / m`**, where `m` = the total number of rows in this ledger at the
   time of the look, *including* the row being spent. So row 1 is judged at 0.1, row 2 at 0.05,
   row 5 at 0.02, and so on. Getting stricter as you look more is the entire point.
3. **A dev-selection grid counts as ONE look** — at the selected config, not at each cell.
4. **Forward-eval rows do not count** as test looks and are recorded as `F#` rows below.
5. **Descriptive characterization rows do not count** and are recorded as `L#` rows.
6. **Rows are immutable.** Correct a row with a *new* row or an appended note, never in place.
7. **Criteria live in the `spec.yaml`** referenced by the row, and are immutable once written.
